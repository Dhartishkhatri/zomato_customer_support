# KB-DELIVERY-003: Late, missing and undelivered orders

**Order still in transit.** If the order status is `out_for_delivery`, no
refund is issued. Share the live tracking link and the delivery partner's first
name. If the order is more than 30 minutes past its promised time, apply a
delay credit of INR 50 to the customer's next order instead of a refund.

**Marked delivered but not received.** Raise a delivery investigation with the
partner. Do not settle a refund on the same conversation; the investigation
closes within 24 hours and refunds follow automatically if the partner cannot
confirm handover.

**Missing items.** Refund the value of the missing items only, not the full
order, unless the missing items are more than half the order value.

**Delivery partner conduct.** Any report of rude, unsafe or abusive conduct by
a delivery partner is escalated to a human immediately, regardless of whether a
refund is involved. These are never handled by automation.
