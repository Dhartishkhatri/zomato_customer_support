# KB-REFUND-001: Refund eligibility and limits

Customers may request a refund for a delivered order when the order is wrong,
incomplete, spilled, or unsafe to eat. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method, except Zomato Wallet orders which are refunded to the wallet.

**Time window.** A refund request must be raised within 48 hours of delivery.
Requests raised after 48 hours are not processed by support and must be routed
to the Grievance Officer queue.

**Amount limits.** A refund can never exceed the amount actually paid for the
order. Partial refunds are issued at the value of the affected items plus a
proportional share of taxes.

**Automation limits.** Support agents may settle refunds up to
INR 500 without a supervisor when the claim is evidenced. Refunds above
INR 1,000 always require supervisor approval. One refund per order: an order
that already carries a completed refund cannot be refunded again.

**Turnaround.** UPI refunds settle in 3-5 business days. Credit card refunds
settle in 5-7 business days. Wallet refunds are instant.
