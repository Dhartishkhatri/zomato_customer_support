# KB-PHOTO-002: Photo evidence for food quality claims

Claims about spoiled, burnt, spilled, foreign-object or visibly wrong food
require a photograph of the delivered item. The photo is checked by the image
authenticity service before any refund is settled.

**Outcomes.**

- *Authentic* - the photo shows a real, unedited capture. The claim proceeds on
  its merits under KB-REFUND-001.
- *Inconclusive* - the checks cannot separate ordinary messaging-app
  re-compression from editing. A supervisor reviews the claim manually. Do not
  tell the customer their photo was rejected.
- *Manipulated* or *AI-generated* - the photo shows editing or synthetic
  generation. No automated refund is issued. The case goes to the Trust &
  Safety queue, which contacts the customer directly.

**Customer communication.** Never accuse a customer of fraud. Never share
forensic scores, heuristic names, or internal confidence values with the
customer. State only that the claim is under review and give a timeline.

**Screenshots and forwards.** A screenshot of a photo, or an image stripped of
camera metadata by a messaging app, is not by itself evidence of tampering.
Ask the customer for the original photo from their camera roll before treating
a low-confidence result as a problem.
